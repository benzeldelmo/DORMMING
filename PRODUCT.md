# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (phone-first; demoed on a laptop and phones on the same Wi-Fi)

## Users

College students and boarding-house tenants in the Philippines who share a dorm room or house. They split groceries, internet, water and rent, and today track it in group chats, notes and mental math. They open DormMing on their phone right after paying for something, or when a bill is about to be due.

Roles: every account is a user; inside a dorm a person is a member or the dorm creator (who can remove members, make a new code, and edit any record).

## Product Purpose

One place for a dorm's money: personal and shared expenses, bill splitting (including an itemized "thermal receipt" split), bills with due dates and repeats, partial payments, and a clear "owed to you / you owe" view. A Gemini-powered assistant (Ming) records entries from plain sentences, but nothing saves without the user confirming. Success: dormmates stop doing manual math and always know who owes whom.

## Positioning

A dorm companion with a character: Ming, a pixel-art orange cat in a green hoodie, reacts to your money situation (happy, worried, celebrating, napping). Splits are exact to the centavo and debts are netted between pairs, so totals never drift.

## Constraints

- Simulated banking only: no deposits, withdrawals, bank links, transfers or real cards. The digital card says so on its face.
- Currency is the Philippine peso, shown as ₱1,000.00.
- OOP 101 class project (MING Dynasty, Team 3, IT 2107); laptop demo on Friday, 13 November 2026.
